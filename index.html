Licensing, not automatic copyright transfer

Your words. Clear boundaries.

These are example licence models to discuss with a buyer. They are not final legal terms; tailor them to each deal and get legal advice for significant releases.

Non-exclusive

Shared-use licence

Suitable when you want to license the same lyric to more than one buyer, subject to the agreed terms.

  • Writer retains copyright ownership
  • Use and distribution permissions defined in writing
  • Credit, adaptations, and royalties agreed upfront
Ask about licensing
Exclusive use

Exclusive licence

Suitable when a buyer wants exclusive permitted use for a defined scope. Exclusivity does not automatically mean copyright ownership transfers.

  • Scope and duration clearly defined
  • Existing licences and restrictions disclosed
  • Fee, credit, royalties, and adaptations agreed
Discuss an exclusive deal
Do not promise “exclusive” rights unless you are entitled to grant them. If you co-wrote a lyric, check the other writer's share and permissions before licensing it.
